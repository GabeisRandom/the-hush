# The Hush

**A language-learning RPG where you talk your way through.** You arrive in the town of Alba with an egg, a surprise for your friend Noa, but Noa is gone, and a gray silence called the Hush is taking people's voices. The little creature that hatches, Mimo, learns words from *you*. Every battle command, every conversation and every quest happens in the language you're learning, so you're using it from the first minute instead of just reading it.

### ▶ [Play The Hush in your browser](https://gabeisrandom.github.io/the-hush/)

🚧 **Early access.** The whole story can be played from the train ride to the credits, in five languages. It's being play-tested right now, so expect rough edges. Fixes and new things land often, and the game updates itself.

## Languages

- Spanish (Latin American)
- Portuguese (Brazilian)
- German
- Chinese (Mandarin), with pinyin or with characters
- Japanese, with rōmaji or with kana and kanji

You can start from zero. The game follows your pace: the faster you pick words up, the more of the story switches into your language. It teaches about 1,800 words per language, up to around a B1 level.

## What's in it

- A big world to explore: Alba and eight regions, and then each of them long ago and in the future
- Turn-based battles where you tell Mimo what to do in full sentences, and good grammar hits harder
- People to talk with freely, a phone with group chats, pen pals, part-time jobs and town debates
- A radio quiz show, a book club and a shelf of stories at your level
- Level exams at the Academy, plus practice papers in the style of real exams
- Side stories for each language: dominoes, Carnaval, a Christmas market, Chinese New Year, a summer festival and more
- Original music for every place and every boss

| Save | P (the game also saves by itself) |

It plays best on a computer with a keyboard, in Chrome or Edge. Phones aren't supported yet.

## Offline play and updates

The first time you open the game, your browser keeps a copy of it, so after that it opens without the internet too. To get it as an app with its own window, click the install button at the right end of Chrome's or Edge's address bar.

When a new version comes out, the game downloads it in the background and shows **"A new version of The Hush is ready"**. Press **Restart now** (it saves your game first), or **Later** to get it the next time you open the game.

## Your saves

There are three save files, kept in your browser on your computer. On the title screen, **Load game → ⬇** saves a copy of a save to a file, to back it up or take it to another computer, and **⬆ Load a save file…** brings one in. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes your saves, so keep a copy.

## Voices

Listening uses your computer's own voices. If a language isn't spoken or sounds robotic, add a voice for it. On Windows, go to **Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices**. You can pick the voice in the journal's Settings.

## About

Everything is made with plain JavaScript and WebGL: every model, place, sound effect and song is generated in code, and the characters, story and music are original. The 3D runs on [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT License).

The exam practice follows the formats of public exams (DELE, CELPE-Bras, Goethe-Zertifikat, HSK and JLPT) with its own questions. The Hush isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the organizations behind those exams.
